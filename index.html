01 / Model the real price

Built for prices that are calculated, not selected.

Index-linked, dimension-driven, recipe-based, contract-specific: if your price is a formula, Pricera runs it, with the governance a spreadsheet will never have.

Index series, banded lookups, decision tables, nested models that share one costing. Monthly LME averages, MOQ floors, "price on request": all native, not scripted. Decimal-exact, down to the cent.

03 / Every number shows its work

A price sales can defend. A quote you can replay.

Each quote line stores the full calculation: model version, index value, table rows, waterfall. Open a quote from last year and replay it. Change an input and the line flags itself stale.

traceability · audit · pinned model version & index snapshot

04 / Continuously recalibrated costing

Quote on today's costs, not yesterday's assumptions.

Pricera prices new and custom products from costing models: forward-looking, built from driver rates, routings and recipes. Those models are continuously recalibrated by the actual costs of the products you've sold. So cost, margin and price stay accurate, for products you quote every week and for products that have never been quoted.

The gap is drafted as a new model version — updated tables, new drivers, missing cost items — and reference cases re-run, so the next quote stands on corrected costs. Nothing publishes without you.
