Why Pricera

Not a pricebook. Not a black box. Not a spreadsheet.

Three ways companies price complex products today, and why none of them is Pricera.

01Not a pricebook

CPQ tools price by list plus discount waterfall; your formulas end up as scripts nobody can read. Pricera makes the formula the product: cost-plus, index-linked, spot and LTA in one engine.

02Not a black box

Price-optimisation AI hands sales a number it can't explain, so sales ignores it. Pricera hands them the derivation. And when AI reads a customer contract, every rule comes with page-level evidence.

03Not a spreadsheet

Excel is transparent to one person and unversioned for everyone else. Pricera keeps the expressiveness and adds versions, tests, roles and a replayable audit trail on every quote.
