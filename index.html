Costing

Quote on today's costs, not yesterday's assumptions.

Pricera prices new and custom products from costing models: forward-looking, built from driver rates, routings and recipes. Those models are continuously recalibrated by the actual costs of the products you've sold. So cost, margin and price stay accurate, for products you quote every week and for products that have never been quoted.

Cost new products

Press hours, energy, scrap, resin recipe, SG&A allocation, all from versioned driver tables. Every pricing model builds on it.

costing models

Check against actuals

Actual cost per sold item lands in Pricera after each close. Driver rates that drifted beyond tolerance are flagged, table by table.

The gap is drafted as a new model version — updated tables, new drivers, missing cost items — and reference cases re-run, so the next quote stands on corrected costs. Nothing publishes without you.

Why Pricera

Not a pricebook. Not a black box. Not a spreadsheet.

Three ways companies price complex products today, and why none of them is Pricera.

01Not a pricebook

CPQ tools price by list plus discount waterfall; your formulas end up as scripts nobody can read. Pricera makes the formula the product: cost-plus, index-linked, spot and LTA in one engine.

02Not a black box

Price-optimisation AI hands sales a number it can't explain, so sales ignores it. Pricera hands them the derivation. And when AI reads a customer contract, every rule comes with page-level evidence.

03Not a spreadsheet

Excel is transparent to one person and unversioned for everyone else. Pricera keeps the expressiveness and adds versions, tests, roles and a replayable audit trail on every quote.
