The problem

Why "AI pricing" makes B2B buyers nervous

The pitch usually goes: feed us your transactions, our models find the optimal price. For an airline seat or a hotel night, fine. For a €70 000 contract with index clauses and an 18-month term, it fails on three counts:

  • Undefendable quotes. A sales rep cannot stand behind a price they cannot decompose. "The model said so" is not an answer to a purchasing manager.
  • Unauditable decisions. When a customer disputes an invoice or an auditor asks how a price was set, an opaque recommendation engine has no answer — and no replay button.
  • Unbounded behaviour. A decider-AI can drift: quietly reprice a live contract, invent a discount no one approved, or chase a margin objective into a customer relationship problem.

The instinct to distrust decider-AI is correct. But it doesn't follow that AI has no place in pricing — it follows that AI belongs in the job where speed matters and accountability is checkable: building the model, not deciding the price.

The distinction

Two jobs for AI: builder, not decider

AI as decider — the black box

  • Outputs recommended prices; the reasoning is hidden
  • Cannot explain a quote to a customer or an auditor
  • Optimizes against its objective, not your contract
  • Trust required; verification impossible

AI as builder — governed by design

  • Drafts the pricing model from your documents and descriptions
  • Every formula is readable, inspectable, owned by your team
  • Reference test cases prove the model before it publishes
  • Prices stay deterministic — and fully replayable

In the builder role, the AI does what it's genuinely good at: reading a 40-page pricing document, extracting the index basis, the adder tables and the clauses, and drafting the model in minutes instead of the weeks a manual build takes. Then it steps aside — a human reviews, reference cases run, and only a passing model goes live.

The rails

What "governed" means in practice

Review before publish

AI-drafted models are proposals, not releases. A pricing owner inspects the generated formulas and tables — readable, not code — and approves or edits before anything reaches sales.

Tests gate every change

Reference cases — known inputs with known correct prices — run on every edit, human or AI. Publishing is blocked until they pass. The model can't silently drift.

Everything is versioned

Each quote pins the model version and index snapshot used. Replay any historical quote; see who or what changed the model and when; roll back if needed.

FAQ

AI-governed pricing, answered

What is AI-governed pricing?

AI-governed pricing is an operating model where AI builds and maintains pricing models — reading documents, drafting formulas and lookups — while governance stays with humans: every change is reviewed, every publish is gated by reference test cases, and every quoted price can be replayed and explained. The AI accelerates the modelling work; it never becomes an unaccountable decision-maker.

How is AI-governed pricing different from AI price optimization?

AI price optimization typically means a machine-learning model that outputs recommended prices sales teams cannot decompose or defend. AI-governed pricing inverts the relationship: the pricing model stays explicit and human-owned, and AI works inside it — drafting models, proposing updates, flagging stale inputs — under tests and approvals.

Can AI safely set contractual B2B prices?

Not as an unsupervised decider. Contract prices carry clauses, index bases and commitments that must be explainable to customers and auditable internally. The safe pattern is AI-assisted modelling: the AI reads the contract and drafts the pricing logic; a pricing owner reviews it; automated reference cases prove the model reproduces known prices before anything goes live.

Is AI-governed pricing the same as dynamic pricing?

No. Dynamic pricing continuously reprices offers in response to market signals — appropriate for seats and rooms, largely wrong for contractual industrial prices. AI-governed pricing keeps prices deterministic and formula-driven; what AI accelerates is the building and maintenance of the pricing model itself.
